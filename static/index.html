KEY TERMS SIMPLIFIED (COMMONLY ASKED DOUBTS)
1. Why is there a green 32×32 box inside a 96×96 image? (FOV vs. ROI)

In the PatchCamelyon (PCam) dataset, a slide is labeled positive only if at least one cancer cell falls inside the central 32×32 pixel square (ROI). The surrounding 96×96 area (FOV) gives tissue context, while our model pools both the whole patch and the center box separately so border cells don't fool it.

2. Why is Youden's J Decision Cutoff 0.126 (12.6%) instead of 50%?

In cancer screening, missing a real tumor (False Negative) is far worse than double-checking a borderline spot (False Positive). Youden's J mathematically finds the threshold on validation ROC curves that maximizes Sensitivity + Specificity − 1. Setting it at 0.126 caught 100% of cancer patches (0 FN) in our 500-patch audit. Dragging Cutoff higher makes the model stricter; lowering it makes it more cautious.

3. What is 4× Rotational TTA & TTA Stability?

TTA (Test-Time Augmentation) rotates the same biopsy 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270° because tissue under a microscope has no "up" direction. Averaging all 4 predictions removes orientation bias. TTA Stability (closer to 100%) means the model gave consistent scores across every angle.

4. What do Grad-CAM & Spatial Attribution (Center Peak vs. Outer Context) mean?

Layer-4 Grad-CAM highlights which cells pushed the AI toward "Metastatic" (red/yellow = strong signal, blue = calm background). Center Peak / Mean measures heat inside the 32×32 target zone vs. Outer Context outside it—helping you confirm the AI looked at actual nuclei inside the ROI.

5. Do 6× HD Lanczos-4 & USM Sharpness change the AI's prediction?

No! Those controls are strictly display optics for human eyes (6× high-res scaling, luminance contrast balancing, and Unsharp Masking edge crispness). The ONNX/PyTorch ResNet-34 model always receives the untouched raw 96×96 pixel patch.

6. What are ROC-AUC, Sensitivity & Specificity in Model Insights?

Sensitivity (100.0%) = out of all true cancer patches, how many we caught (204/204). Specificity (97.97%) = out of all healthy patches, how many we cleared (290/296). ROC-AUC (0.9998) measures overall ranking separation across all cutoffs (1.0 is perfect).

7. How is Tab 03 (3D Biomarker Explorer) different from Tab 01?

Tab 01 analyzes raw H&E microscope images using PyTorch CNNs. Tab 03 is a separate tabular Random Forest model trained on 10 measured cell-nucleus numbers (radius, concavity, perimeter) from the Wisconsin dataset—move sliders or presets to see how cell geometry shifts risk in 3D space.
